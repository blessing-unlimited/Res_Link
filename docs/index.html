Stakeholder Ecosystem

Who It Is For

Designed to support every key role within the student living environment.

Students

Stay updated with official residence announcements, RSVP to social events, and submit maintenance tickets directly from their phones.

Admin

Streamline residence notices, manage check-in registers, coordinate room allocations, and broadcast alerts across halls.

Security

Access immediate emergency communication protocols, log gate incidents, and verify authorized resident entries in real time.

Maintenance

Receive live task dispatches for plumbing, electrical, and room repairs with clear priority queues and resolution status updates.

Core Functions

Four Pillars of the Platform

Key pillars that make residence living organized and connected.

01

Community

Interactive noticeboards and social forums connecting residents across floors, encouraging peer support and camaraderie.

02

Events

Residence calendar featuring study groups, sporting tournaments, game nights, and official hall meetings with easy RSVPs.

03

Safety

One-touch emergency contacts, security check-ins, and rapid safety alerts ensuring peace of mind across the entire estate.

04

Maintenance

Transparent maintenance ticketing system allowing fast fault logging, photo attachments, and prompt resolution tracking.
